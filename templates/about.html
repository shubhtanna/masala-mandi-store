Our story

From a mandi shop to your kitchen

In 1987 Ramesh Agrawal opened a small spice stall in Siyaganj, Indore's wholesale market. He knew the farmers by name, tasted every lot, and refused to sell anything he wouldn't cook with at home.

His grandchildren started Masala Mandi online in 2020, during the lockdown, when regular customers in Pune and Bengaluru kept calling to ask if we could post their garam masala. We still buy whole spices from the same mandis, clean and sort them by hand, and grind in small batches every week.

Our masala blends are family recipes. Nothing is added: no colour, no starch, no salt, no anti-caking agents. Every batch is tested at an NABL-accredited lab.
